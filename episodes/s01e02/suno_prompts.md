scene: late-night library stacks during finals week, green desk lamp, campus quad through the window  
tempo range: 80–86 bpm · palette: mellow rhodes, boom-bap drums, upright bass, vinyl crackle, tape warmth

## how to use

- Generate in Suno **on a paid plan** (commercial rights only cover songs made and downloaded on a paid plan). Pro = 20 downloads/month, Premier = 60/month, Suno Studio downloads on Premier are uncapped. Re-check Suno's terms before each release.
- Turn on **Instrumental**. Paste the style line into *Style of Music*; paste the exclude line into *Exclude Styles* if your plan has it.
- Aim for 2:30–4:00 per track. A 60-minute mix needs ~20 keepers — there are 25 prompts here, keep the best ~20.
- Download, then drop the files into `episodes/s01e02/tracks/` and run `lofi scan s01e02`. Name files the way you want the chapter titles to read (lowercase), or set the title tag.
- Never add artist names or ask for samples of real records. Tracks used in another episode can't be reused — `catalog.csv` blocks it.

**exclude styles (all prompts):** `lyrics, rap vocals, trap hi-hats, 808 rolls, EDM drops, autotune, harsh distortion`
