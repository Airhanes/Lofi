# title template

```
{title stem} soulful lofi beats [1 hour]
```

- all lowercase (proper nouns too — "hbcu", not "HBCU")
- the stem names a real place and time: "after hours at the barbershop", "sunday morning in grandma's kitchen"
- one emoji at the end of the stem
- keep the whole title under 70 characters so it isn't truncated in search (YouTube's hard limit is 100)

**thumbnail text** is 2–4 words taken from the stem, set in the display font, e.g. `after hours` / `barbershop at night`. Two variants per episode (A and B) for Test & Compare — vary the words *or* the composition, not just the color.
