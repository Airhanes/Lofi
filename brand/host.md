# recurring host — character sheet

Two options for the owner to choose from. Set `host: A` or `host: B` in `config.yaml`; until then art prompts are written without the host and scenes can be props-only. Both hosts are original characters — not modeled on any real person or any other channel's mascot.

Shared across both options:
- **Props that follow the host:** a walnut record player, a trailing pothos plant, a worn black-and-white composition notebook with a cracked spine.
- **Energy:** absorbed and at ease — studying, listening, sketching, sipping something warm. Never posing for the camera, never performing.
- **Rendering:** warm practical light on the skin with rich, accurate tone in highlights *and* shadows; no greyed or ashy shadows, no lightening in golden light.
- **Reject the image if:** skin reads lighter than described, hair texture is loosened/straightened/smoothed, features are exaggerated, or clothing reads as costume.

---

## option A — "dee"

A young Black woman in her mid-twenties.

| | |
|---|---|
| skin | deep brown skin with warm red-gold undertones |
| hair | shoulder-length locs, medium thickness, natural texture visible, sometimes half-up with a wooden hair cuff |
| face | soft round face, full lips, small gold hoop earrings |
| wardrobe | oversized chunky-knit mustard cardigan over a cream tee, wide-leg dark denim, house socks |
| signature | oversized retro headphones (walnut-and-cream), resting on her neck when not listening |
| habits | writes lyrics and lists in the composition notebook; taps a pencil on the beat; flips records |

**Prompt fragment**
> a young Black woman with deep brown skin and warm undertones, shoulder-length natural locs with visible texture, small gold hoops, oversized retro headphones, a chunky mustard cardigan over a cream t-shirt

**Motion ideas for loops:** gentle head nod on the beat, pencil tapping, turning a notebook page, breathing, locs shifting as she leans.

## option B — "jules"

A young Black man in his mid-twenties.

| | |
|---|---|
| skin | deep brown skin with cool-neutral undertones |
| hair | low fade with tight natural coils on top, a full, neatly lined beard |
| face | strong brow, warm eyes, thin gold chain |
| wardrobe | vintage varsity jacket — forest green body, cream sleeves, a chenille letter patch with no real school's logo — over a plain tee |
| signature | over-ear headphones, worn on, one ear pushed back when he's reading |
| habits | sketches in the composition notebook; digs through records; waters the pothos |

**Prompt fragment**
> a young Black man with deep brown skin, a low fade with tight natural coils on top, a full neatly lined beard, over-ear headphones, a vintage forest-green varsity jacket with cream sleeves and a plain chenille letter patch

**Motion ideas for loops:** head nod, sketching hand, flipping through a record bin, steam rising from his mug, a slow breath and shoulder shift.
