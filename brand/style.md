# style guide

The channel is a place, not a genre tag: warm, specific, everyday Black life rendered with love — cozy, nostalgic, a little cinematic. Every scene should feel like a real place someone remembers. Never a costume, never a punchline.

## look

- **Light:** golden-hour amber and lamplight against deep indigo nights. One warm practical light source per scene (lamp, neon, stove, TV, porch bulb) doing most of the work; the rest falls off into blue shadow.
- **Palette:** see `palette.md`. Warm accents (amber, burnt orange, mustard) live in the light; cool tones (indigo, plum, forest green) live in the shadows and the night outside.
- **Texture:** film grain, soft halation around lights, slightly lifted blacks — the warmth of a 70s soul record sleeve. No hard digital sharpness, no neon-cyberpunk.
- **Composition:** eye-level or slightly high, medium-wide, a lived-in foreground (props on a table) with a window or doorway as the background. Leave the lower-left third calm enough for the now-playing card and the thumbnail title.
- **Recurring props:** record player, pothos plant, worn composition notebook. At least one appears in every scene; the record player is the default "spinning thing."
- **People:** the host (see `host.md`) appears relaxed and absorbed in something — studying, listening, working. Name skin tone and hair texture explicitly in every image prompt.

## motion (non-negotiable: real motion, not a still)

Every loop needs at least two of: rain or droplets on glass, steam, flickering light, a spinning record, drifting dust or lightning bugs, the host moving (head nod, page turn, breathing). A slow zoom alone does not count. Loops are 8–20 s and seamless.

## sound

The jazz → soul → gospel → neo-soul lineage over boom-bap. Rhodes and Wurlitzer, warm upright or electric bass, swung off-grid drums, gospel organ swells, muted trumpet or sax, vibraphone, wordless soul vocal textures, vinyl crackle, tape warmth. 68–90 BPM. Prompts describe instruments and feel — never artist names, never samples of real records.

## words

- Lowercase titles: `{title stem} soulful lofi beats [1 hour]` (see `title_template.md`).
- One emoji per title stem, at the end of the stem.
- Plain, warm language. No slang-stuffed titles, no dialect played for effect.

## guardrails

- Skin tone and hair texture are named in every image prompt; reject outputs where skin drifts lighter or hair texture flattens (straightened, loosened curl pattern, smoothed locs).
- No caricatured features, no stereotypes used as props or jokes (no watermelon/fried-chicken iconography, no "hood" set dressing, no costume clothing).
- Don't imitate another channel's signature character or scene composition (no study-girl-at-desk-by-window-with-cat framing lifted from existing lofi channels).
- The owner approves every scene (`art.approved`) and every tracklist (`music.approved`) and writes every director's note.
